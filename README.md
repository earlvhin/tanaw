# Tanaw
 
**Natapos ba talaga ang proyekto sa lugar mo?**
 
Tanaw is a civic social network prototype that helps Filipinos keep an eye on government projects in their own towns. Find the DPWH projects near you, see what the government says about them, then go and check. Post a photo or video of what's really there, and everyone can compare the official "100% tapos" with what the people saw.
 
- Browse 246,000+ DPWH projects by town and barangay
- See the budget, contractor, bidders and signed contract documents
- Report with geotagged photos and videos in four taps
- Confirm others' reports with "Nakita ko rin" and discuss each project together
Project data comes from the [DPWH Transparency Portal](https://transparency.dpwh.gov.ph) (November 2025 snapshot).
 
*Concept prototype. Not affiliated with DPWH or any government agency.*
